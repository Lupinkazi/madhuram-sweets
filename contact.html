Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Choose products on the order page, add your details and send the request. The shop confirms prices, availability and timing with you before anything is final.

Do your sweets contain nuts or dairy?

All our sweets are made with ghee, many contain milk, and some contain nuts. Please ask about any specific product before ordering if you have an allergy.

How should I store sweets?

Storage advice depends on the sweet. Ask at the counter or contact us for guidance on a specific product.

Are your sweets and food 100% vegetarian?

Yes. Madhuram Sweets & Snacks is a 100% pure veg shop and restaurant, adhering strictly to authentic vegetarian preparation standards.

Do you deliver, and which areas do you serve?

We serve the Kathmandu Valley. Choose your delivery area on the order page, and the shop will confirm whether delivery or collection suits your location.

Can I pick up my order from the shop?

Yes. Mention collection in the message box of your order and visit us at Dhungedhara, Kathmandu once the shop confirms it is ready.

Do I pay online when I place an order?

No. No payment is taken on the website. The shop confirms the prices with you and tells you how to pay.

Can I order through WhatsApp or by phone?

Yes. You can send your order list through WhatsApp from the order page, or call the shop on any of the numbers listed above.

How far in advance should I order for festivals or celebrations?

Festival and wedding seasons are busy, so we suggest ordering as early as you can. Add your preferred date and time in the order message, and the shop will confirm if it can be met.

Can you pack sweets as gifts or for large orders?

Please tell us your packing request and the quantity in the order message or contact form. The shop will confirm what it can arrange.

Can I see prices and pack sizes before ordering?

Browse our menu page for all sweets, meals, snacks and available pack sizes. Prices and availability are confirmed by the shop when it replies to your order.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday to Saturday from 9:00 am to 9:00 pm, and on Sunday from 10:00 am to 8:00 pm.
