About us

Sweets made with patience, shared with pride

We are a family-style sweets and pure vegetarian restaurant kitchen focused on traditional recipes, careful preparation and the joy of sharing something good.

Our beginning

Where it all started

Madhuram Sweets & Snacks started with a small shop in Dhungedhara, Kathmandu, and a simple idea: to make traditional ghee sweets the way they have always been made — fresh, by hand, and with good ingredients. What began as a small counter soon became a familiar place for local families. People visited us during festivals, special occasions, and even on ordinary days when they simply wanted something sweet. Many became regular customers because they loved the taste and knew what to expect every time they visited.

Our popular sweets, such as Motichur Laddu, Jalebi, Besan Laddu, and Barfi, are made using traditional methods that have been passed down through years of experience. These skills were learned in the kitchen, by watching, practising, and working together. From roasting gram flour to getting the right colour and texture, every step takes time and care. As Madhuram has grown, we have stayed true to the way we started. We still believe that good sweets need fresh ingredients, careful preparation, and a little patience. That is what we have always stood for, and what we continue to offer our customers every day.
