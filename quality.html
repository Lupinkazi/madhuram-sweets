Ingredient standards

What goes into our sweets

Every ingredient is chosen to meet the standard our recipes demand — no shortcuts, no substitutes.

  • Ghee, milk & paneer

    Pure cow and buffalo dairy, fresh cottage cheese and rich cream delivered fresh.

  • Flour, sugar & grains

    Carefully sifted gram flour, whole wheat flour, premium basmati rice, and quality sweeteners.

  • Spices & nuts

    Green cardamom, saffron, cashews, pistachios, and fresh whole spices ground in-house.

  • Allergen information

    Our kitchen prepares dishes containing dairy, nuts, and gluten. Please ask our team about specific allergens.

Production process

From kitchen to plate

  1. Selection

    Ingredients are checked when they arrive.

  2. Preparation

    Recipes are cooked and set in batches.

  3. Finishing

    Sweets are cut, shaped and garnished by hand.

  4. Packing

    Boxes are packed and labelled for the counter or orders.
